# Vision

SDC is not a single product.

It is a business ecosystem.

Every feature, AI capability, marketplace item, course, and integration should reinforce the others through a flywheel that increases customer value, retention, and recurring revenue.

---

# Core Principles

The monetization strategy should:

* Maximize customer lifetime value (LTV).
* Maintain healthy gross margins.
* Keep AI costs predictable.
* Reward engagement.
* Encourage upgrades.
* Support partners and creators.
* Scale internationally.
* Be transparent to users while abstracting infrastructure complexity.

# 4. Creator Credit Economy

Credits abstract infrastructure costs into a simple experience.

Users spend credits for:

* Chat
* Images
* Video
* Voice
* Translation
* Business plans
* Long-form content
* Specialized AI agents

Credits are:

* Monthly allocations
* Purchasable as add-ons
* Awarded through promotions
* Potentially earned via referrals or community programs (subject to anti-abuse rules)

Credits are never described in terms of tokens.
